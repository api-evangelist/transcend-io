Transcend was recognized by Snowflake in the Privacy & Consent category of its Modern Marketing Data Stack report as a "One to Watch" vendor. The autonomous data decision platform encodes data-use permissions into enterprise systems that process customer data, providing real-time consent and preference governance. Transcend and Snowflake jointly enable marketing teams to activate customer data with built-in permission controls across the MarTech and AdTech ecosystem.
